Hikaru Hatsumi's dream is to become the world's greatest Pokémon pop idol.
She's a talented singer with a kind heart, good grades, and a love for bubble tea and
all things Pinap Berries.

After graduating from Gale Beach High School, Hikaru joined Project Flameback alongside
her classmates [Bruce](/characters/bruce-flameback), [Sammy](/characters/sammy-pepperoni),
and [Walter](/characters/walter-fisthands), on their mission to discover the source of
the spacetime rifts that have been appearing across the Alchas region.

Hikaru is agile in battle, using Trailblaze (which she taught herself from a TM) to raise her
Speed stat and then dealing Speed-boosted damage with Electro Ball.
She's also caring and intelligent, having excelled in physics class and developed her
business acumen while working summers at her mom's surf shop, Alola Sporting Goods.

Hikaru was born to a mixed family: her dad is [Goku Hatsumi](/characters/goku-hatsumi),
an Infernape from the legendary Hatsumi clan of the Vulcanburg area, and her mom is
[Janis](/characters/janis-hatsumi), an Alolan Raichu from Briney Breeze Island.
She also has a younger sibling, a Chimchar named Quinn. Together, they live on the second
floor of their beach house on the western edge of town, just above the Alola Sporting Goods
store.

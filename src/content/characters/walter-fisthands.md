A kid thrown into the world after escaping his household of his abusive stepfather,
Vincent the Vigoroth, and his absent rockstar mother, Veronica the Lucario.
Walter now goes through life finally thinking for himself and
doesn't want to be the kid pushed around like he was in school, so he acts tough, but
inside, he is still the scared kid he was before graduation.

Walter can use his Inner Focus ability to sense auras in the space around him, which
enabled him to feel that something was off when the blue Orb went missing.
He carries an electric guitar that he brought from home, as well as blocks of TNT that
he got from Vickers the Octillery during a mission in Vulcanburg.

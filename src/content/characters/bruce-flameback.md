Though he is the son of the owners/founders of Flameback Enterprises, a very successful
corporation, he was shy and had only one close friend growing up:
[Sammy Pepperoni](/characters/sammy-pepperoni). This is because (until recently) he
believed he had lost his father in an accident 8 years ago and since then he has become
very closed off and suspicious of almost anyone. He is very protective of his adopted
younger sister Arabella, who was found abandoned shortly after his father's disappearance,
and as such was very dedicated to battle training in high school. Since our story began,
he has become much closer to Sammy's friends [Hikaru](/characters/hikaru-hatsumi) and
[Walter](/characters/walter-fisthands) (in that he now considers them friends).

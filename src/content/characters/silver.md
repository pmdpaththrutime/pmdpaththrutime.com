Silver is a misguided, young Shiny Espeon. Her goal in life is to make everything just
and right in the world. Her means of doing so are not the most ethical, and this has led
to her making more than a few enemies. However, her search for power has garnered the
eye of many followers who seek to take a portion of that power for their own, gaining
Silver her followers. Silver is very direct, and not very nice. She gets what she wants
and doesn't care what it takes to get there.

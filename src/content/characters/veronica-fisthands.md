Veronica is the mother of [Walter](/characters/walter-fisthands) and she loves him with
all her heart. She is the co-lead singer of the rock band The Gravelrocks, along with
Jeremy the Midnight Lycanroc. Though she wants nothing more than to provide for her son,
she seems to keep a lot of secrets behind those mascara-lined eyes.

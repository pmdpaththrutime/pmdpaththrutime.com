Salena is a cute but self-absorbed Glaceon who loves to hang out with her friends, two Umbreon named Abyss and Brielle.

Her goal is to go forwards in time and save her mother from a horrible accident that occurs in 20 years.

Salena wears a red bandana that was given to her by her mother, and never takes it off.

She thinks she's the best one in the room at all times, and she won't stop at anything to achieve her goals, even if it means getting her hands dirty.

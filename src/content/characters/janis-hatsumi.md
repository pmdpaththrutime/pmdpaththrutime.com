Janis Hatsumi (née Onda) is [Hikaru](/characters/hikaru-hatsumi)'s mom and the owner of
Alola Sporting Goods, a surf shop on the ground floor of the Hatsumi family's beach house.
When she's not running the store, she's a professional surfer who competes in surfing
competitions around the Alchas region.

Originally from Briney Breeze Island, Janis returns there every year for the annual
Alchas World Surfing Competition, in which she has won seven medals over her surfing
career.
